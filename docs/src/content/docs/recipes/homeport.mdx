A next-bun-compile binary runs anywhere: a VPS, a container platform,
Kubernetes, your own hardware. The [VPS](/next-bun-compile/recipes/vps-deploy/)
and [Docker](/next-bun-compile/guides/docker/) guides cover running it
yourself. Nothing in the package depends on any host.

[Homeport](https://homeport.sh/) is one hosted option, made by the same
author. It deploys Next.js apps, including ones built with
next-bun-compile, with no Dockerfile, no systemd unit and no CDN worker
to write. Your app doesn't change: build it as in
[Getting started](/next-bun-compile/getting-started/), then follow
[homeport.sh](https://homeport.sh/) to deploy it.

## What it does for the binary

- **Serves pages from its edge.** Homeport speaks the
  [edge contract](/next-bun-compile/guides/edge-ppr/#the-edge-contract):
  it reads the build's `edge.json`, stores the static pages and PPR
  shells it lists, and sends only the dynamic holes to your app.
- **Purges on revalidation.** A `revalidateTag` or `revalidatePath`
  reaches the edge at once through
  [`CDN_PURGE_URL`](/next-bun-compile/guides/edge-ppr/#instant-purge-cdn_purge_url),
  instead of on its next check.

On Homeport you don't set either variable:

- **`EDGE_TOKEN`**: Homeport creates one for your app on its first
  release whose `edge.json` lists pages. It keeps the same token across
  releases and shows it in your app's variables. If you set your own,
  Homeport keeps yours; it must be 32–128 lowercase hex characters, or
  the edge won't serve your pages and every request goes to the app.
- **`CDN_PURGE_URL`**: Homeport gives it to every process when it starts
  (web, workers, cron and the release step), as a private address only
  your app can reach. It isn't stored in your variables. Apps served
  under a path prefix, or with no domain, don't get one; the binary then
  skips the purge without an error.

Running more than one copy with on-demand revalidation? Give every copy
a shared cache handler; see
[Custom cache handlers](/next-bun-compile/configuration/#custom-cache-handlers).
