---
title: Deploy with Homeport
description: Homeport deploys Next.js and next-bun-compile apps without setup, and serves their PPR shells and static pages from its edge.
---

[Homeport](https://homeport.sh/) deploys Next.js apps, including ones
built with next-bun-compile, without any setup on your side: no
Dockerfile, no systemd unit, no CDN worker to write.

What it does for a next-bun-compile app:

- **Serves pages from its edge.** Homeport speaks the
  [edge contract](/next-bun-compile/guides/edge-ppr/#the-edge-contract):
  it reads the build's `edge.json`, stores the static pages and PPR
  shells it lists, and sends only the dynamic holes to your app. It sets
  `EDGE_TOKEN` for you.
- **Purges on revalidation.** It sets
  [`CDN_PURGE_URL`](/next-bun-compile/guides/edge-ppr/#instant-purge-cdn_purge_url),
  so a `revalidateTag` or `revalidatePath` reaches the edge at once
  instead of on its next check.

Nothing in your app changes: build it as in
[Getting started](/next-bun-compile/getting-started/), then follow
[homeport.sh](https://homeport.sh/) to deploy it.

Running more than one copy with on-demand revalidation? Give every copy
a shared cache handler; see
[Custom cache handlers](/next-bun-compile/configuration/#custom-cache-handlers).
